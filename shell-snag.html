How to play

3-Minute Run

Collect shells and chase your best score before the timer ends.

Endless

Survive harder waves every minute. Fresh shells, +10 health, then 10 seconds before crabs arrive.

Controls & tips +

Keyboard & mouse

WASD Move · Shift Sprint

Space Jump · F / click Attack

Q Sword swipe · M Music

Controller & touch

Press a controller face button once to connect it. On mobile, use the on-screen controls.

Click the game to capture your mouse. Press Esc to release it or leave full screen.

Sign in on your profile to earn automatically. Each collected shell earns one Clam; shell colors give different game scores. Your full-run Clams are paid when the run ends.
